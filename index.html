STOCKIPA

I got tired of watching my mom (CJ) hustle hard as a mtumba seller and not know where her money went. So I built her an app that actually works.

Bill Scanning

Sell a pen for 10 bob? Scan the cash. It records. Simple.

M-Pesa Auto-Tracking

Send money? It logs as expense. Receive money? It logs as sale. No manual entry needed.

Smart Tracking

See daily, weekly, monthly, yearly. Charts, search, calendar. Know exactly where your money goes.

Stock Management

Add items, track buying price, selling price, quantity. Low stock alerts. Know your profit per item.

Offline Free Secure Fast Multi-Language
Click to zoom

📈 The Stockipa Journey Real Results

From May, the user (my mom) was overspending expenses outpaced income every week.
But as the months went by, with consistent tracking, she started to reduce overspending.
By August, money in exceeded expenses, and she was able to save more each month.

This is what Stockipa does. Know where your money goes, and watch your savings grow.

Home Page

Add sales/expenses manually or scan bills. M-Pesa auto-recorded. See if you saved or overspent today.

Tracking Page

Daily, weekly, monthly, yearly views. Yearly chart. Calendar picker. Search. See your performance.

Stock Management

Item name, buying price, quantity, selling price. Low stock alerts. Know your profit.

Settings

Switch language (English, Kiswahili, Chinese currency auto-switches). Light/dark mode. Clear data.

TapTaner

🚰 3D Smart-Tap Simulation

Source Live

I noticed a real problem at Chuka University's Muthaiga hostels: analog taps can't close themselves. When a student walks away and leaves one open, litres of water vanish every day. TapTaner is a 3D simulation of a smart tap that detects flow and auto-closes — a proof-of-concept for retrofitting old taps with cheap sensors instead of replacing them.

Interactive 3D tap model
Auto-close on no-presence
Retrofit, don't replace
