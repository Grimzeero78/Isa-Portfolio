Future Project

Power Beaming.

I have been obsessed with this idea since I read about space-based solar power. Sending electricity through the air, from 1 km to orbit. Cutting the last wire.

The Vision

We beam data across the planet at light speed. We beam images from Mars. But to send power 5 kilometers to a remote village, we still string copper on wooden poles. We can do better.

Why This Matters To Me

Space-based solar power is not sci-fi. NASA, ESA, JAXA, and China's CASIC are all working on it. This is the ultimate clean energy, baseload power, 24/7, from orbit.

Tech & Roadmap

📡

5.8 GHz Microwave

Punches through rain, fog, dust. 0.1-10 km range.

🔦

850nm-1550nm Laser

Tight beam. 1-100 km range. Clear sky only.
